🏥 Community Health Data Collection & Analysis
📌 About This Project
Community Health Data Collection & Analysis is a healthcare data analytics project designed to collect, organize, analyze, and visualize community health information.

The system analyzes demographic details, lifestyle habits, physical measurements, health conditions, and other health-related factors to identify patterns and provide meaningful insights into the overall health status of a community.

The collected data is transformed into an interactive Power BI dashboard, allowing users to explore health trends through charts, KPIs, filters, and visual reports.

🎯 Objective
The main objectives of this project are:

Collect and organize community health-related data
Analyze demographic and health-related information
Identify common health patterns and risk factors
Calculate and visualize important health indicators such as BMI
Compare health conditions across different groups
Present insights through an interactive dashboard
Support better understanding of community health trends
📌 Project Overview
This project focuses on community-level health data analysis using data visualization and business intelligence techniques.

The system takes structured health data as input, performs data cleaning and analysis, and presents the results through an interactive Power BI dashboard.

🏥 Domain
Healthcare / Health Data Analytics / Data Visualization

❓ Problem Statement
Community health data is often available in large amounts, but analyzing it manually can make it difficult to identify important patterns.

Some of the challenges include:

Large Amount of Health Data
Community health information may contain demographic details, physical measurements, lifestyle factors, and medical conditions that are difficult to analyze manually.

Difficulty Identifying Health Patterns
Relationships between factors such as age, BMI, physical activity, sleep, water intake, diabetes, blood pressure, and other health conditions may not be immediately visible.

Lack of Interactive Visualization
Raw datasets and spreadsheets do not provide an easy way to explore health information from different perspectives.

Need for Meaningful Health Insights
Without proper analysis and visualization, it can be difficult to understand the overall health condition of a community.

✅ Our Solution
The Community Health Data Collection & Analysis project addresses these challenges by:

Collecting structured community health data
Cleaning and preparing the dataset for analysis
Calculating important health indicators
Analyzing demographic and lifestyle factors
Visualizing health conditions using Power BI
Providing interactive filters for detailed analysis
Displaying key health statistics through KPI cards
Identifying patterns and trends from the collected data

📊 Project Features
Feature 1 – Health Data Collection
Collects and organizes structured health information from community participants for further analysis.

Feature 2 – Data Preprocessing
Cleans and prepares the collected data by handling missing or inconsistent values and creating calculated health indicators.

Feature 3 – Health Indicator Analysis
Analyzes important indicators such as BMI, blood pressure, diabetes, cholesterol, physical activity, sleep, and other health-related factors.

Feature 4 – Interactive Dashboard
Provides an interactive Power BI dashboard where users can explore community health information using charts, KPIs, and filters.

Feature 5 – Demographic Analysis
Allows health information to be analyzed based on demographic characteristics such as age and gender.

Feature 6 – Lifestyle Analysis
Analyzes lifestyle factors including physical activity, smoking, alcohol consumption, sleep duration, and water intake.

Feature 7 – Health Condition Analysis
Visualizes the distribution of conditions such as diabetes, heart disease, blood pressure status, and cholesterol status.

Feature 8 – Overall Health Insights
Provides a summarized view of community health patterns and helps identify areas that require further attention.
